Learn from the Best

At DoBu Martial Arts, we believe that great instructors make great students. Our team of highly qualified, passionate coaches brings decades of combined experience in Judo, Jiu-jitsu, Karate, Muay Thai, and sports science. Each instructor is dedicated to helping you unlock your full potential — whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned athlete.

Meet the Team

Get to know the experts who will guide you every step of the way.

Mauricio Gomez

Gym Owner & Head Martial Arts Coach

4th Dan Judo 3rd Dan Jiu-jitsu 1st Dan Karate Muay Thai Coach

Coaches in all martial arts disciplines. With over 20 years of experience, Mauricio is the driving force behind DoBu's world-class training programs.

Sarah Nova

Assistant Martial Arts Coach

5th Dan Karate Kata Specialist Sparring

A master of traditional karate, Sarah specializes in kata, sparring techniques, and youth development. Her energy and precision inspire students of all ages.

Guy Victory

Assistant Martial Arts Coach

2nd Dan Jiu-jitsu 1st Dan Judo Ground Specialist

An expert in ground techniques and takedown defense, Guy brings a tactical, detail-oriented approach to every class. He's passionate about helping students master the fundamentals.

Morris Davis

Assistant Martial Arts Coach

Muay Thai Coach 3rd Dan Karate Striking

Accredited Muay Thai coach and 3rd Dan Karate blackbelt. Morris specializes in striking, power development, and conditioning. His classes are intense, fun, and results-driven.

Traci Santiago

Fitness Coach

BSc Sports Science Health & Nutrition Strength & Conditioning

BSc in Sports Science and qualified in health and nutrition. Traci specialises in devising strength and conditioning programs for combat athletes of all levels.

Harpreet Kaur

Fitness Coach

BSc Physiotherapy MSc Sports Science Injury Prevention

BSc in Physiotherapy and MSc in Sports Science. Harpreet is an expert in injury prevention, recovery, and rehabilitation. She helps athletes stay strong, healthy, and performing at their peak.
