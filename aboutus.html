Our Story

DoBu Martial Arts was founded in 2010 with a simple but powerful vision: to create a space where people of all ages and backgrounds could discover the transformative power of martial arts.

What started as a small studio in Yangon with just a handful of students has grown into a thriving community of over 500 active members. Our journey has been driven by a relentless commitment to excellence, safety, and personal growth.

Today, DoBu is recognized as one of Yangon's premier martial arts academies, offering world-class instruction in Judo, Jiu-jitsu, Karate, and Muay Thai — all under one roof.

Our Mission

Our Vision
